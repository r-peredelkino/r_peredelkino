The engineering selection boundary did not include the whole visible external pavement. `publicRealmLayout.boundary` is its union with the reconstructed external forecourt; it is separate from the immutable building/engineering traces. Neighborhood street geometry is still diagrammatic, with named roads and compass orientation, rather than surveyed GIS. The extra frontage depth and furniture sizes are estimates from the image.

The same geometry is used in registration, resident genplan and walking mode. Curved seats, planter islands, pavilion posts/tables and storefront entrances participate in collision. Added tour places: promenade, office square and forest edge. Ground-level walking is restricted to the pedestrian public envelope. Existing apartment selection, chat, home controls, seasons, weather and traffic remain.

All new geometry and materials pass through the existing baked attribution pipeline for @r_peredelkino, including pixel and geometry signatures. Historical v13 exports are preserved. CPU geometry/layout, illumination state, traffic phases, walking collision and attribution checks are performed; this environment does not provide an actual iPhone/WebGL performance measurement.
